Structured Patient Care

UROLOGY CONSULTATION PROCESS

We follow a transparent, multi-step clinical pathway to ensure thorough evaluation and personalized care planning.

01

Initial Consultation

Detailed discussion regarding your symptoms, health expectations, and reason for visit.

02

Medical History

Comprehensive review of prior medical conditions, family health history, and current medications.

03

Clinical Assessment

Physical examination and clinical evaluation conducted in a private consultation room.

04

Investigations

Ordering lab diagnostic testing (urinalysis, blood panels, PSA) or imaging where appropriate.

05

Treatment Planning

Explaining diagnostic findings and developing an individualized treatment or lifestyle guidance plan.

06

Follow-up Care

Scheduled review consultations to monitor progress and adjust treatment as necessary.
