About WMC Worldwide Medical Center

WMC Polyclinic is a healthcare facility in Al Garhoud, Dubai, providing a range of medical and healthcare services for individuals and families. We believe healthcare should involve listening to patients, understanding their concerns and explaining the appropriate next steps clearly.

As a full-service polyclinic, we blend state-of-the-art medical technology with compassionate, client-centered care. Our medical team consists of experienced Physicians, Surgeons, Rehabilitation Specialists, and allied healthcare professionals dedicated to prevention, clinical diagnostics, and community wellness.

  • Patient-Centred Communication & Transparent Guidance
  • DHA-Approved Clinical Systems & Advanced Equipment
  • Multidisciplinary Specialist Team Committed to Excellence
  • Continuous Support from Preventive Screening to Recovery
