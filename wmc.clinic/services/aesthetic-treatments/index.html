Patient Safety & Guidance

BEFORE YOUR TREATMENT

Patients can discuss treatment suitability, expected outcomes, possible risks, number of sessions, preparation and aftercare with the treating professional prior to undertaking any aesthetic procedure.

Medical History & Skin Evaluation

Every aesthetic journey begins with a thorough clinical consultation to evaluate skin sensitivity, existing medical conditions, and previous cosmetic procedures.

Consultation Expectations

  • Skin Assessment: Detailed analysis of skin type, pigmentation, and target area suitability.
  • Realistic Goals: Professional explanation of achievable aesthetic outcomes.
  • Safety & Risks: Clear discussion of procedure steps, transient side-effects, and precautions.
  • Aftercare Plan: Post-treatment sun protection, skincare guidelines, and follow-up timing.
