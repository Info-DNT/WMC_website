PRIVACY & PATIENT POLICY

WMC Polyclinic LLC – Patient Confidentiality, Data Protection & Clinic Standards

1. Privacy Commitment & Entity Identification

WMC Polyclinic LLC ("WMC", "we", "us", or "our") operates the medical facility located at Suite 310, 3rd Floor, Dubai Autism Center, Garhoud Community, Al Garhoud, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

We are committed to maintaining the highest ethical and legal standards of healthcare privacy, confidentiality, and data protection in accordance with United Arab Emirates health regulations, Dubai Health Authority (DHA) guidelines, and applicable federal laws.

2. Collection of Personal & Health Information

To deliver comprehensive medical consultations, diagnostic services, pediatric care, urological consultations, and aesthetic assessments, WMC Polyclinic LLC collects relevant personal and medical details.

Types of Information Collected:

  • Personal Identification Details: Full name, Emirates ID number, date of birth, gender, nationality, and contact details (phone number, WhatsApp, email address).
  • Medical History & Consultation Data: Health records, previous clinical assessments, lab diagnostic results, prescription details, and attending physician notes.
  • Appointment & Communication Records: Booking requests submitted via our website, telephone calls, WhatsApp messages, or in-person reception logs.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

All patient medical records and personal health data are stored in secure, password-protected, encrypted clinical management systems. Access is strictly limited to authorized attending healthcare professionals and administrative personnel involved in your care.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal medical information to third-party commercial marketers under any circumstances.

4. Patient Rights & Responsibilities

As a patient of WMC Polyclinic LLC, you are entitled to:

  • Receive clear, respectful, and compassionate medical care from qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Access your diagnostic lab reports and medical records upon verified request.
  • Receive transparent explanations of diagnostic procedures, care plans, and fees prior to treatment.
  • Maintain complete confidentiality regarding your health status and treatment options.

5. Appointment Scheduling & Cancellation Guidelines

To ensure optimal scheduling for all patients, we request at least 24 hours' notice for appointment rescheduling or cancellations. You may cancel or reschedule by calling our clinic reception at +971 4 570 7960 or messaging our verified WhatsApp account at +971 58 133 2355.
