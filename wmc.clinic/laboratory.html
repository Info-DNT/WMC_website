AVAILABLE LABORATORY TESTS

We facilitate a wide range of diagnostic investigations to assist in medical evaluation, chronic disease monitoring, and routine preventive health checkups.

Blood Tests

Complete Blood Count (CBC), ESR, Hemoglobin, Blood Grouping, Anemia Panel, and blood cell morphology investigations.

Diabetes Tests

HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin), Fasting Blood Glucose, Random Blood Sugar, and Insulin sensitivity screening.

Lipid Tests

Full Lipid Profile including Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL (Good Cholesterol), LDL (Bad Cholesterol), and VLDL.

Liver Function Tests

Liver Panel evaluating ALT (SGPT), AST (SGOT), Bilirubin, Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP), Total Protein, and Albumin.

Kidney Function Tests

Renal Panel assessing Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN), Uric Acid, Serum Electrolytes, and Routine Urinalysis.

Hormone & Vitamin Tests

Thyroid Profile (TSH, Free T3, Free T4), Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, PSA screening, and general hormonal evaluations.

Other Approved Diagnostic Tests

In addition to core profiles, our laboratory partner supports specialized investigations including inflammatory markers (CRP), infectious disease screening, allergic reaction panels, and routine pre-employment health screening.

Patient Guidance

BEFORE YOUR LABORATORY TEST

Patients should contact WMC Polyclinic to confirm test availability, fasting requirements, preparation instructions, sample collection procedure, and result turnaround times.

Key Preparation Steps

  • Fasting: Certain blood tests (e.g., Fasting Glucose, Lipid Profile) require 8 to 12 hours of fasting prior to sample collection.
  • Medication: Inform our staff if you are taking prescribed medications prior to blood collection.
  • Hydration: Drink adequate water unless instructed otherwise by your attending doctor.
  • ID Verification: Bring your original Emirates ID or Passport for official registration.
