Child Healthcare Scope

PEDIATRIC SERVICES

Our pediatric department offers comprehensive healthcare support designed to meet your child's physical, developmental, and emotional healthcare needs.

Pediatric Consultations

Routine and acute medical consultations tailored for newborns, infants, children, and teens.

Growth Monitoring

Tracking height, weight, head circumference, and physical growth percentiles over time.

Development Assessment

Evaluating motor skills, speech development, cognitive milestones, and social progress.

Childhood Illnesses

Diagnosis and management of acute pediatric conditions like fever, cough, colds, and ear discomfort.

Wellness Consultations

Routine wellness checkups, nutritional guidance, and advice on healthy childhood habits.

Vaccination (Where Offered)

Immunisation guidance and routine childhood vaccination scheduling where available.

Preventive Care

Proactive health screening, seasonal protection, and hygiene consultation for growing children.

Chronic Condition Support

Ongoing consultation and management support for pediatric asthma or allergies (where offered).

Developmental Tracking

GROWTH & DEVELOPMENT

Children develop at different rates. During an appropriate pediatric consultation, parents can discuss growth, development, nutrition and other healthcare concerns with our qualified medical team.

Milestone Tracking Support

From infant feeding and weight gain to adolescent wellness, our consultations focus on supporting parents with clear, reassuring clinical guidance.

When Should You Consult a Pediatrician?

Parents may consider scheduling a pediatric consultation for:

  • Recurring fever, cough, flu, or digestive upset
  • Concerns about physical growth or developmental milestones
  • Dietary habits, appetite changes, or nutrition concerns
  • General routine wellness or school entry checkups
  • Other childhood health concerns or symptoms
