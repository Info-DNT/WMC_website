Clinical Scope

PREVENTIVE HEALTHCARE SERVICES

Our preventive services are designed to evaluate overall health, track key physiological indicators, and support proactive lifestyle and clinical decisions.

Routine Health Consultations

Regular clinical consultations to discuss general health, address new symptoms, and maintain continuous medical records.

Health Assessments

Comprehensive clinical evaluations including vital signs, BMI tracking, cardiovascular risk screening, and medical history reviews.

Preventive Screening

Targeted screenings for early detection of potential metabolic, cardiovascular, or organ-system variations.

Laboratory Investigations

Diagnostic laboratory panels (blood count, glucose, lipid profile, liver & kidney tests) processed through approved lab partners.

Chronic Disease Monitoring

Structured monitoring for ongoing conditions like hypertension, high cholesterol, or early glucose intolerance.

Lifestyle Discussions

Evidence-based advice on nutrition, physical activity, sleep hygiene, stress reduction, and tobacco cessation.

Vaccination (Where Available)

Routine adult and seasonal immunisation guidance to safeguard against preventable infectious diseases.
