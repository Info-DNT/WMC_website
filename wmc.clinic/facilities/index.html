Diagnostics

IN-HOUSE CLINICAL LABORATORY

Our on-site clinical laboratory provides rapid diagnostic turnaround times, high analytical accuracy, and extensive testing panels for blood, urine, and metabolic health.

Laboratory Capabilities

  • Complete Blood Counts (CBC): Automated hematology analyzers for fast infection screening.
  • Biochemistry & Metabolic Panels: Glucose, lipid profiles, liver function, and kidney function tests.
  • Hormone & Endocrine Profiling: Thyroid profiles, vitamin levels (D3, B12), and hormone assays.
  • Urinalysis & Cultures: Rapid detection of urinary tract infections and renal screening.
