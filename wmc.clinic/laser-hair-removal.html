Aesthetic Science

HOW LASER HAIR REMOVAL WORKS

Laser hair removal utilizes concentrated pulses of light absorbed by the pigment (melanin) in hair follicles. The light energy converts to heat, reducing the follicle's ability to produce new hair while protecting surrounding skin.

Progressive Growth Cycle Treatment

Because hair grows in distinct phases (anagen, catagen, and telogen), light energy is most effective on follicles in the active growth phase. Consequently, a series of scheduled sessions is required to achieve gradual hair reduction.

Clinical Considerations

  • Individual Response: Effectiveness depends on hair color, skin tone, area, and hormonal factors.
  • Session Intervals: Sessions are typically spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart based on the body area treated.
  • Cooling Systems: Medical laser devices incorporate skin-cooling mechanisms to optimize comfort during treatment.
  • Patch Testing: Conducted prior to full treatment to assess skin sensitivity and parameters.

LASER HAIR REMOVAL FOR MEN

Laser hair removal may also be suitable for men. Common areas may include the back, chest, shoulders and other approved areas, subject to assessment.

Patient Safety Instructions

BEFORE & AFTERCARE PROTOCOLS

BEFORE TREATMENT

Follow preparation instructions provided by the healthcare professional. Requirements can vary by treatment area and technology.

  • Shave Target Area: Shave the area 12 to 24 hours prior to treatment so energy targets the follicle root under the skin.
  • Avoid Sun Exposure: Avoid active sun tanning, sunbeds, or self-tanning creams for 2 weeks before treatment.
  • No Plucking / Waxing: Do not pluck, wax, or use depilatory creams for 4 weeks prior, as the follicle root must be intact.
  • Clean Skin: Ensure the treatment area is free of lotions, deodorants, perfumes, or makeup on treatment day.

AFTER TREATMENT

Follow the clinic’s aftercare instructions and contact WMC if you have concerns.

  • Apply Soothing Gel: Use pure Aloe Vera or recommended soothing gel if mild redness or warmth occurs.
  • Avoid Heat Exposure: Avoid hot showers, saunas, steam rooms, and intense exercise for 24 to 48 hours.
  • Daily Sun Protection: Apply broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen to any sun-exposed treated areas.
  • Gentle Skincare: Avoid harsh exfoliants, chemical scrubs, or perfumed products on treated skin for 3–5 days.
