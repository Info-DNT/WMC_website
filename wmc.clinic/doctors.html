DHA Licensure & Verification

All medical practitioners at WMC Polyclinic are fully licensed by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA) and undergo continuous professional development to ensure high standard clinical care.

Why Choose WMC Physicians

  • Multi-Lingual Consultations: Consultations available in English, Arabic, Hindi, Tagalog, and Urdu.
  • Comprehensive Evaluations: Thorough medical history reviews and appropriate diagnostic referrals.
  • Collaborative Care: Seamless internal cross-referrals between GP, Urology, Pediatrics, and Diagnostics.
  • Patient Privacy: Strict medical record confidentiality and patient privacy protocols.
